01 — Consult

We Listen
First

Discovery call

A 30-minute conversation to understand your goals, timeline, and budget — no pressure, no hard sell.

Site visit

We walk through your home together, taking measurements and noting what's possible within your space.

Brief alignment

We document every requirement and preference before a single design is drawn.

Great renovations start long before the first tool is picked up.
